TvNinja is a minimalist IPTV player for M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts, built with Material You design.

TvNinja does not provide any channels: it plays the playlists you add.

Features:

- M3U/M3U8 playlists from a URL
- Xtream Codes login for Live, VOD and Series
- Browse the public iptv-org playlists by country, language or category (the adult category is hidden)
- Favorites on the home screen, search and group filtering
- Audio-only mode with background playback and media notification controls
- Pauses automatically when headphones disconnect
- Picture-in-picture when leaving the fullscreen player
- Persistent mini-player while browsing the app
- Dark and light Material You themes with dynamic color
- Localized in English, Italian, French, German, Spanish and Japanese
- No ads, no tracking, no Google Play Services, fully open source
